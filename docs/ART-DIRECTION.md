# Dirección de arte · versión 0.2

Se incorporaron directamente las cuatro imágenes aportadas por el usuario: portada, lámina de colección, atlas de 18 personajes y hoja de carrera. Las copias originales están en dist/assets/reference/. No se han sustituido por nuevas interpretaciones. Los recortes son coordenadas de renderizado; los originales conservan sus píxeles y transparencia.

Luffy y Zoro usan sus cuatro fotogramas de carrera. La galería muestra los 18 diseños de la referencia. La campaña conserva los cinco personajes y cinco capítulos de East Blue. Se generaron Nami, Usopp y Sanji clásicos, más los enemigos, para completar el mismo estilo sin introducir poderes posteriores en la campaña.

Herramienta: ImageGen integrada. Archivos finales: dist/assets/classic-chibi.png y dist/assets/enemies-chibi.png.

## Prompt final · tripulación clásica

Use case: game character sprite sheet. The attached image is a STYLE REFERENCE, not an edit target. Create a NEW transparent sprite sheet matching its compact chibi pixel-art One Piece game style: large expressive heads, small sturdy bodies, crisp dark pixel outlines, limited cel shading, clearly stepped pixel edges, about 100-pixel native-detail characters. Single row of THREE separate full body characters, evenly spaced, all feet on same baseline with transparent margins and generous gutters. Left: Nami from EARLY EAST BLUE, short orange bob hair, white shirt with horizontal blue stripes, orange skirt and brown boots, holding a plain wooden staff. Center: Usopp from EARLY EAST BLUE, long nose, black curly hair, brown bandana with goggles, brown overalls and cream sash, boots, holding his small wooden slingshot. Right: Sanji from EARLY EAST BLUE, blond fringe covers his left eye, visible spiral eyebrow, black double-breasted suit over blue shirt, black tie, black shoes, poised fighting stance. Match proportions and pixel texture of the reference Luffy and Zoro. NO fire, NO energy, NO later saga clothing, NO long-haired Nami, NO green slingshot, NO text, NO labels, NO scenery, NO checkerboard. Actual transparent background. Complete silhouettes, nothing cropped.

## Prompt final · enemigos

Use case: game enemy sprite atlas. Attached image is STYLE REFERENCE for compact chibi pixel art sprites, not an edit target. Create a NEW transparent atlas of EIGHT One Piece East Blue enemies in exactly TWO rows and FOUR columns. Equal size cells, large transparent gutters, full bodies, feet baseline aligned inside every cell. Large expressive heads and small stout bodies, crisp dark pixel outlines, stepped pixel edges, rich limited cel shading matching reference. TOP ROW left to right: Axe-Hand Morgan (short blond hair, metal jaw, white Marine coat, striped shirt, steel axe replacing right forearm); Buggy (blue hair side tails, orange pirate hat, red round nose, red-white striped shirt, pirate trousers, knives); Captain Kuro (slick black hair, glasses, black butler suit, white shirt, teal tie, ten long metal claws); Don Krieg (lavender hair, huge gold armor, red cape, white fur collar, heavy boots). BOTTOM ROW left to right: Arlong (blue sawshark fishman, jagged nose, black hair, pale fur cap, yellow floral shirt, green shorts); ordinary Marine sailor (white cap, white shirt with blue collar, blue trousers, holding short cutlass); Buggy pirate band member (red bandana, striped shirt, brown trousers, cutlass); ordinary Arlong fishman (teal skin, broad fishlike face, simple dark vest, short trousers, unarmed). All face toward viewer, neutral battle-ready poses, all weapons remain inside their cell, no overlap. NO lettering, NO names, NO labels, NO background, NO battle effects, NO gore. Actual alpha transparency.
